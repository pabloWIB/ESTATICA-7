Reference board

PARADOX

Time is a limited and irreplaceable resource, so use the time to stay focused and motivated as you work towards your goals.

Every second you spend working towards your goals brings you one step closer to achieving them, so don't let any time go to waste. This is a board of 32 pieces — posters, type specimens, identity and web design — collected around that one idea. Nothing here was made by me: each piece is credited to the designer who made it, and the collection exists to study how other people have argued the same point.

About & credits

Whose work this is

PARADOX is a study board, not a portfolio of my own image-making. The pieces were collected because of how they handle a single argument about time; they remain the property of their authors. Where a designer signed the work, the signature is reproduced below. If you are one of the authors and want a piece removed, the contact address at the foot of this page reaches me directly.
